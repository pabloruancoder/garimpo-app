Delete your Garimpo account

Last updated: September 25, 2026
Português · English · Español

In the app

Open Garimpo, tap your photo at the top to open Profile, then tap "Delete my account". Deletion is immediate.

By email

Without the app installed, email logvpcampus@gmail.com from your Google account address with the subject "Delete my account". We delete it within 7 days and reply to confirm.

What is deleted

Your account (Google name, email and photo), open sessions, favorites, alert preferences, the groups the account followed, and the comments and photos you posted.

None of it is kept after deletion. The random device identifier stays in the app until you uninstall it, with no link to you.
