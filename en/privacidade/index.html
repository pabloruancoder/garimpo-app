Privacy policy

Last updated: September 25, 2026
Português · English · Español

Summary

Garimpo collects the minimum it needs to work and does not sell your data. This policy follows the LGPD (Lei Geral de Proteção de Dados, Brazil's General Data Protection Law, Law 13.709/2018).

Without an account

The app creates a random identifier for your device that does not reveal who you are. It is used to remember the groups you follow and your alerts.

If you allow notifications, we store the device notification code to let you know when a group is about to end or has closed.

With an account

When you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email and photo. We do not receive your password and we never post on your behalf.

We also store what you do in your account: favorites, alert preferences and the comments and photos you post.

Price search

Price search runs on your own device, directly on the store's website. The terms you search for stay only on your phone, in the recent searches list, which you can clear at any time.

When you open or share a product, the address of that listing is sent to our server to create the affiliate link.

What we do not collect

We do not collect your location, your contacts, your purchases at the stores or payment data. Photos in your gallery are only read when you pick one for a comment.

Who we share with

Google, for sign-in and to deliver alerts (Firebase Cloud Messaging). Stores (such as Mercado Livre and AliExpress), when you open one of their links: from then on, the store's privacy policy applies.

We do not sell or rent your data to anyone.

Storage and security

Data is stored on the Garimpo server. Your account session expires after 90 days, and the access code is stored securely on your device.

Your rights

You can request access to, correction or deletion of your data, and deletion of your account, by email at logvpcampus@gmail.com. Signing out in the app turns off the account's alerts on that device.

Age

The app is intended for people over 18, who are able to shop online.

Changes to this policy

This policy may change. The date of the last update is shown at the top of this page.
